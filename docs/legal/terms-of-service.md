## Service

True Margin Tracker calculates estimated and exact product profitability from store, order, cost, fee, ad, refund, and return data provided by connected platforms and merchant input.

## Merchant Responsibilities

Merchants are responsible for:

- Entering accurate product costs, return assumptions, shipping rules, and integration credentials.
- Confirming that connected store, payment, advertising, and catalog data can be lawfully processed.
- Reviewing margin outputs before making pricing, inventory, or advertising decisions.
- Keeping API tokens, signing secrets, license keys, and account credentials secure.
