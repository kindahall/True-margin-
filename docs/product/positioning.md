# Product Positioning

## Market Position

True Margin Tracker is not a broad e-commerce analytics suite. It is a focused, lower-cost profitability tool for Shopify and WooCommerce merchants worldwide.

## Target Customer

Small and mid-sized merchants selling physical products globally:

- Shopify stores.
- WooCommerce stores.
- Solo operators and lean teams.
- Merchants who use Meta Ads, TikTok Ads, Google Ads, PayPal, Stripe, and carrier/shipping rules.
- Merchants who need a fast answer, not a complex analytics platform.

## Promise

Know which products are truly profitable after product cost, shipping, payment fees, platform fees, ad spend, refunds, and returns.

## Differentiation

The product should be:

- Simpler than large analytics suites.
- Cheaper than enterprise-grade reporting platforms.
- Faster to understand than a full BI dashboard.
- Focused on product-level profit decisions.
- Usable worldwide with configurable currencies, gateways, taxes, shipping rules, and return assumptions.

## Not The Product

Avoid building another generic analytics platform. Do not compete feature-for-feature with large tools. True Margin Tracker should win by being focused, clear, affordable, and quick to set up.

## Language

The app interface is English-first. All merchant-facing copy, dashboard labels, alerts, setup screens, Shopify embedded screens, and WooCommerce admin screens must be in English.

## Pricing Direction

Keep the entry plan affordable:

- Starter: one small store, up to 1,000 orders/month, manual costs and CSV imports.
- Growth: several sales channels, up to 10,000 orders/month, payment fees and exports.
- Pro: team or high volume, up to 50,000 orders/month, API and team access.

Exact prices can change, but the product should remain materially cheaper and simpler than heavy analytics suites.

## Research Snapshot - May 2026

The Shopify profit-tracking market is real but crowded. Comparable tools include Lifetimely, BeProfit, TrueProfit, Polar Analytics, Triple Whale, ClearMargin, Clarifi, and newer lean apps. Several competitors market the same core pain: revenue is not profit, and merchants need COGS, shipping, payment fees, ads, refunds, and returns in one view.

Observed pricing anchors:

- Lean new tools often start around free to $19-$20/month.
- Established Shopify analytics tools can rise sharply with order volume.
- Lifetimely's Shopify listing shows higher paid tiers tied to monthly orders.
- Merchant discussions repeatedly mention cost, order-based pricing, and distrust of inconsistent profit numbers as pain points.

Product implication:

- Do not position as "another Shopify analytics app."
- Lead with "simple real margin after every cost" and a cheaper predictable plan.
- Avoid per-order pricing that feels like a tax on growth.
- Make setup visibly faster than large analytics suites.
- Keep the no-data state honest until a real store/plugin is connected.

Best wedge:

- WordPress/WooCommerce plus direct-license sales is less saturated than the Shopify-only market.
- Shopify should begin as direct install/private distribution from the owner site.
- Public Shopify App Store distribution needs a separate Shopify Billing path.
